Prompts for AI agents

 
An agent is an AI that does several steps by itself (search, read, write, run code). Give it a goal, limits, and a way to finish.

 






 
What NOT to use AI for

 
AI is powerful, so misuse can hurt real people and get you into serious trouble. Never use these tools for the things below.

 

 
Rules and laws differ by place and keep changing. When in doubt, don't do it, and ask a teacher or parent.

Prompt tips: do and don't

 
Do
  • Say who you are (class, language) and what you want.
  • Ask for a format: points, table, steps.
  • Give one example of what you like.
  • Ask follow-ups: "simpler", "give an example", "why?".
  • Ask the AI to say when it is not sure.

 
Don't
  • Don't write one vague word.
  • Don't paste private details.
  • Don't trust numbers, dates or quotes without checking.
  • Don't copy the whole answer into homework.
  • Don't ask for harmful or unfair things.
